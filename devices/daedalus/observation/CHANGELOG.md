## v2.0.2 — 2026-08-02

### Fixed

- Correctly extract publication dates from ESA/Webb Picture of the Month pages.

## v2.0.1 — 2026-08-02

### Changed

- Updated the Hubble provider from the discontinued Picture of the Week feed to the new Picture of the Month feed.

---

## v2.0.0 — 2026-08-02

### Added

- NASA APOD
- ESA/Hubble support
- ESA/Webb support
- Source selector
- Automatic refresh
- Favourite images
- Source page links
- Remember last selected provider

---

## v1.0.0 — 2026-08-02

### Added

- NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day integration
- Standard and high-resolution image downloads
- Local caching outside the Git repository
- Bronze-on-navy Engraved Unicode renderer
- ImageMagick and Chafa rendering pipeline
- Renderer playground and presets
- Floating GTK 4 Observation panel
- Clickable preview that opens the full-resolution image
- Waybar launcher with a monochrome satellite icon
- Automatic silent refresh at login
- Offline-safe behaviour when NASA cannot be reached
